# AP Language assessment design

Create original materials aligned to the actual task being practiced, not a device-identification worksheet labeled as an exam. Read [references/patterns.md](references/patterns.md) for prompt patterns, project design, and the verified source register. Recheck the official CED and current exam page before asserting current exam specifications. The reference review date is October 28, 2026.

## Design decisions

- Establish a concept, a measurable reading move, a corresponding writing move, and a defensible relationship to a CED skill code. Use the four big ideas and paired reading/writing categories; distinguish official codes from editorial concept names and unit titles.
- Reading questions should ask what a choice does in this passage, with enough context to support the inference. Revision questions need a stated rhetorical goal and an editable sentence or passage.
- Build one best answer before distractors. Derive plausible distractors from wrong function, scope, referent, causal relationship, or a partial reading. Avoid joke options, implausible trivia, conspicuous length differences, and always placing the answer first. Explain why every option succeeds or fails with passage evidence.
- For a rhetorical-analysis essay, provide an actual complete passage, writer, audience, occasion, and purpose. Ask for an interpretation of choices and their contribution. Do not demand a predetermined device list or agreement with the writer.
- For synthesis, supply the entire packet: six usable sources, multiple perspectives, source labels and context, and quantitative/visual material. Require an original argument drawing on at least three sources. Original simulated sources are acceptable for teaching if labeled prominently; never present invented people, data, or publication details as real research.
- For argument, frame an open question with a genuinely contestable position. Demand specific evidence and explanations. Do not invent a famous person's quotation to make the prompt look official. A short thesis need not be nuanced to satisfy the thesis row; do not award sophistication for an ornamental concession.
- Use the official 0–1 / 0–4 / 0–1 essay dimensions with task-specific rules. Link full scoring guidelines; a short teaching guide must not pretend to reproduce all decision rules. Do not equate a classroom percentage, project grade, or six-point essay score with the five-point AP exam result.
- Label short isolated items as focused practice. Exam-style structure is not evidence of calibrated exam difficulty. Distinguish extended original essays, paragraph scaffolds, and genuine exam simulations.

## Projects

Combine original composition, visual or spatial argument, rhetorical self-analysis, a supported position, revision, and reflection in one coherent inquiry. Give each concept its own meaningful dilemma and artifact. Explain why the visual medium matters. Use observable work across Bloom's cognitive categories without treating them as a mandatory one-way staircase.

Specify audiences, workload, evidence expectations, deliverables, and criteria. Have students quote their own choices and explain mechanism → plausible audience effect → purpose, then test an alternative. Offer low-material and text-accessible options. Assess visual reasoning, not expensive materials or artistic polish. Do not assign a fixed voice to an ideology or personality category.

For libraries, reuse a complete stimulus when it helps compare skills, but disclose the reuse and count assignments separately from distinct prompts/passages. Make every concept's practice and project reachable, include source provenance, and distinguish prerequisite relationships from transfer or complementary links.

## Review

Check every claim, item, key, distractor rationale, source label, quantitative calculation, cross-reference, and prerequisite. Audit for missing materials and false certainty. Use student attempts or teacher review to refine difficulty; do not claim validation from a syntax or completeness test. Preserve the user's source artifact and explain significant adaptations without rewriting it unasked.
